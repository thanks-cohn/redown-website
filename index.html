05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you connect.

Does REDOWN store my files on its own servers?

REDOWN is designed to transfer supported files into storage you connect, such as your own Cloudflare R2 account. For supported remote saves, REDOWN transfers the file to your connected storage without downloading it to your computer first. REDOWN does not supply separate storage space.

Does every download avoid my computer?

No. The direct-transfer pathway is available for supported sources and files. Other operations, including local uploads or browser-assisted download paths, may use your device. REDOWN should indicate the relevant workflow.

Is Google Drive already supported?

Not yet as a released integration. Google Drive is planned. The currently described cloud destination is Cloudflare R2.

Can I use REDOWN without an R2 account?

Cloudflare R2 must be available on your Cloudflare account for the current R2 workflow. Storage usage may incur charges from Cloudflare. Please review your provider's pricing and permissions before connecting.
